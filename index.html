THE HEART OF OUR ACADEMY

Rooted in art.
Growing together.

A nurturing space for creative beginnings, personal growth and a lifelong love for the arts.

01

Our Vision

To create an inspiring and nurturing environment where children discover their purpose, explore their unique potential, and understand the positive role they can play in society. Through meaningful artistic experiences, we aim to guide and shape confident, compassionate, and capable future citizens.

02

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality music, dance, and arts education that supports the holistic development of every child. Through creative expression and structured learning, we strengthen cognitive, language, physical, and social skills while building confidence, character, and a lifelong love for the arts.

ANUSHA SURYAS FOUNDER & EDUCATOR

FOUNDER & EDUCATOR

Anusha SuryasAn artist’s dedication.
An educator’s care.

FOUNDER, ANUSHA ART ACADEMY · MUSIC & KUCHIPUDI EDUCATOR

For Anusha, the arts are as much about nurturing people as they are about learning a skill. With over 12 years of experience teaching music through individual and group classes, online and in person, she brings an artist’s dedication and an educator’s care to her practice.

A disciple of Shri K. Ramachari and an alumna of Little Musicians Academy, she has trained in music and Kuchipudi at Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University, Hyderabad. Her background in early childhood education shapes the academy’s thoughtful approach to learning.

12+ years Music teaching experience
Gold medallist Early Childhood Education, Osmania University
Training, education & recognition +
  • M.Tech, JNTU Hyderabad; B.Ed, Rayalaseema University.
  • Diploma and certification in Kuchipudi from Telugu University, Hyderabad.
  • South India Women Achievers Award, Art & Culture category, 2026.
  • Delhi Telugu Academy Award, 2018.
  • Best Dance Teacher award from Telangana State Sangeeta Nataka Akademi, 2019.
  • All India Radio artist and judge for IMA Sargam, 2021.

Her teaching work includes individual and group learners in India and abroad.

Can adults join or start learning again?+

Yes. Adults are welcome to reconnect with an artistic journey or begin a new one. Ask the academy about the right class and batch for you.

Where can I check timings and fees?+

Select a class above to see its weekly timings. For fees, current availability and upcoming batch launch dates, please contact the academy. All listed timings are in Indian Standard Time.

Sankeertanas workshops run Monday–Thursday, 10:30 a.m.–12 noon, in small batches based on enrolments. Sunday music, dance and art workshops are also enrolment-based. Contact us for the next dates, activities and fees.
